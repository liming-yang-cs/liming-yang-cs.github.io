01 / FEATURED PROJECTLLM EVALUATION · MENTAL HEALTH

CrisisInteract

Evaluating Interactional Robustness in LLM-Based Mental Health Crisis Response

A controlled, multi-turn framework for testing how clinician LLMs respond when a patient is not straightforward to help. An interactional difficulty controller introduces low engagement, avoidance, ambivalence, and resistance while keeping profiles and crisis contexts matched.

Controlled evaluationMulti-turn dialogueResponsible AI
KEY FINDING ↗
1.13–1.43 point drop on a 5-point appropriateness scale

A 23–29% reduction under interactional difficulty across four clinician LLMs. Avoidance was significantly associated with degraded performance.

6 crisis categories · 60 patient profiles · 480 ten-turn interactions↗
